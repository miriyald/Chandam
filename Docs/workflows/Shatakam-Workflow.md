# Telugu శతకం Planning Guide

A comprehensive workflow for planning a Telugu శతకం (Shatakam) — a collection of padyams unified by a common మకుటం (refrain) and theme. This guide covers brainstorming, chandam selection, మకుటం design, sectional architecture, and per-padyam concept planning.

> **Scope:** This workflow focuses on **planning** the శతకం — identifying concepts, ideas, and key elements for each padyam with logical flow. For actually composing individual padyams, refer to the composition phases within this guide.

---

# Part I: Knowledge

Reference material for Telugu శతకం composition. Consult these sections as needed during planning.

---

## 1. What is a శతకం?

A **శతకం** (literally "a hundred") is a collection of standalone yet thematically unified padyams (verses), all sharing the same:

- **ఛందస్సు** (meter) — every padyam follows the same chandam
- **మకుటం** (crown/refrain) — a recurring word, phrase, or line that appears in every padyam, typically at the end of the last line

### Historical Significance

శతకం is one of the most beloved forms in Telugu literature. It allows a poet to explore a theme from many angles — each padyam is complete in itself, yet together they form a unified philosophical or devotional statement.

### Famous Examples

| శతకం | Author | Theme | ఛందస్సు | మకుటం |
|-------|--------|-------|---------|--------|
| వేమన శతకం | వేమన | నీతి / తత్త్వం | ఆటవెలది | విశ్వదాభిరామ వినురవేమ |
| సుమతీ శతకం | బద్దెన | నీతి | కందం | సుమతీ |
| భర్తృహరి నీతి శతకం | భర్తృహరి (ఏనుగు లక్ష్మణకవి అనువాదం) | నీతి | చంపకమాల / ఉత్పలమాల | భర్తృహరీ |
| భర్తృహరి వైరాగ్య శతకం | భర్తృహరి | వైరాగ్యం | చంపకమాల / ఉత్పలమాల | భర్తృహరీ |
| దాశరథీ శతకం | కంచెర్ల గోపన్న (రామదాసు) | భక్తి | చంపకమాల / ఉత్పలమాల | దాశరథీ |
| నరసింహ శతకం | శేషప్ప కవి | భక్తి | సీసం + ఆటవెలది | నరసింహా |
| కాళహస్తీశ్వర శతకం | ధూర్జటి | భక్తి / వైరాగ్యం | ఉత్పలమాల / చంపకమాల | శ్రీ కాళహస్తీశ్వరా |

## 2. మకుటం (Refrain)

The మకుటం is the signature element that gives a శతకం its identity. It appears in every padyam, binding the collection together.

### మకుటం Types

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Last word/phrase of last line** | Most common. A word or short phrase ends the final line | "సుమతీ" (సుమతీ శతకం) |
| **Last half of last line** | A longer phrase occupying the second half of the last line | "విశ్వదాభిరామ వినురవేమ" (వేమన శతకం) |
| **Full last line** | The entire last line repeats in every padyam | Some భక్తి శతకాలు |
| **Vocative address** | A calling/address to the deity or subject | "దాశరథీ" , "నరసింహా", "శ్రీ కాళహస్తీశ్వరా" |

### మకుటం Design Constraints

The మకుటం must satisfy these requirements **in every single padyam**:

1. **Metrical fit** — The మకుటం's guru/laghu pattern must align with the last line's gaNA pattern at the position where it appears
2. **ప్రాస compatibility** — If the మకుటం starts the last line or occupies a fixed position, it constrains what ప్రాస consonants are possible
3. **యతి compatibility** — If the మకుటం falls at or near the యతి position, the preceding word must satisfy యతి with it
4. **Grammatical flexibility** — The మకుటం must work grammatically with 108 different preceding contexts. Vocative forms ("ఓ నరసింహా!") are easiest because they work as standalone addresses

### మకుటం Position Analysis

For a given chandam, analyze where the మకుటం sits:

```
Example: వేమన శతకం in ఆటవెలది
Last line pattern: 5 gaNAs (odd line in ఆటవెలది)
మకుటం "విశ్వదాభిరామ వినురవేమ" occupies the last ~3 gaNAs
The poet has only the first ~2 gaNAs of the last line for new content per padyam
```

**Key insight:** A longer మకుటం is easier to maintain (less variation needed per padyam) but limits expressive freedom in the last line. A shorter మకుటం (single word like "సుమతీ") gives maximum freedom but requires more skill to integrate naturally.

## 3. Chandam Choices for శతకం

Not all meters are equally suited for శతకం. The best meters for శతకం allow:
- Natural placement of the మకుటం
- Enough room per padyam for a complete thought
- Sufficient variety in word choice across 108 verses

### Commonly Used Meters

| ఛందస్సు | Type | Lines | Difficulty | మకుటం Flexibility | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| **ఆటవెలది** | ఉపజాతి | 4 | Easy | High | Most popular for నీతి శతకాలు. No ప్రాస required between all lines |
| **తేటగీతి** | ఉపజాతి | 4 | Easy | High | Similar flexibility to ఆటవెలది |
| **కందం** | జాతి | 4 (2 pairs) | Medium | Medium | Compact, punchy. Great for నీతి. OddNonJa and Sixth rules add complexity |
| **ఉత్పలమాల** | వృత్తం | 4 | Hard | Medium | Majestic, 20 syllables/line. Best for భక్తి/వైరాగ్యం |
| **చంపకమాల** | వృత్తం | 4 | Hard | Medium | 21 syllables/line. Often paired with ఉత్పలమాల |
| **సీసం + ఆటవెలది/తేటగీతి** | ఉపజాతి | 4+4 | Medium-Hard | Very High | 8 lines total. Maximum expressive room per padyam |
| **మత్తేభవిక్రీడితము** | వృత్తం | 4 | Hard | Medium | Grand meter, less commonly used for full శతకం |

### Mixed-Meter శతకాలు

Some famous శతకాలు use **two meters alternately** (e.g., దాశరథీ శతకం alternates between ఉత్పలమాల and చంపకమాల). This is valid and gives variety, but the మకుటం must fit both meters.

## 4. Padyam Count Conventions

| Count | Tradition | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| **108** | Most traditional | Considered sacred (అష్టోత్తర శతం) |
| **100** | Common | Round number, simpler |
| **101** | Sometimes used | 100 + 1 dedicatory verse |
| **Flexible** | Modern | Any reasonable count, theme-driven |

Typically the first 1-2 padyams are **ఆవాహన** (invocation) and the last 1-2 are **ఫలశ్రుతి** (benediction/fruit of reading).

## 6. Syllable Basics: Guru and Laghu

Every Telugu syllable is classified as either **guru** (heavy/long) or **laghu** (light/short). This is the foundation of all meter analysis.

| Type | Telugu | Symbol | Description |
|------|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Guru** | గ | `U` | Heavy/long syllable (2 మాత్రలు) |
| **Laghu** | ల | `\|` | Light/short syllable (1 మాత్ర) |

**What makes a syllable guru:**
- Long vowels: ఆ, ఈ, ఊ, ఏ, ఐ, ఓ, ఔ
- Anusvara (ం) on the syllable
- Visarga (ః) on the syllable
- A syllable followed by a conjunct consonant

**What makes a syllable laghu:**
- Short vowels: అ, ఇ, ఉ, ఋ
- No anusvara, no visarga, no following conjunct

## 7. GaNA Classification

Each Telugu letter in a chandam rule represents a specific pattern of guru and laghu syllables.

### Tri-syllable GaNAs (3 aksharas each)

| Telugu Symbol | Name | Binary Pattern | Mnemonic |
|---------------|------|----------------|----------|
| న | na-gaNamu | `\|\|\|` | all light |
| స | sa-gaNamu | `\|\|U` | light-light-heavy |
| య | ya-gaNamu | `\|UU` | light-heavy-heavy |
| జ | ja-gaNamu | `\|U\|` | light-heavy-light |
| భ | bha-gaNamu | `U\|\|` | heavy-light-light |
| ర | ra-gaNamu | `U\|U` | heavy-light-heavy |
| త | ta-gaNamu | `UU\|` | heavy-heavy-light |
| మ | ma-gaNamu | `UUU` | all heavy |

### Sub-units (1-2 aksharas)

| Telugu Symbol | Binary Pattern | Description |
|---------------|----------------|-------------|
| గ | `U` | Single guru |
| ల | `\|` | Single laghu |
| వ | `\|U` | laghu + guru (2 syllables) |
| హ | `U\|` | guru + laghu (2 syllables) |
| గా | `UU` | Two gurus |
| లల | `\|\|` | Two laghus |

### Reading a Chandam Rule Pattern

When a chandam rule says the pattern is `భ ర న భ భ ర ల గ`, expand it:

```
భ  = U||
ర  = U|U
న  = |||
భ  = U||
భ  = U||
ర  = U|U
ల  = |
గ  = U
```

Full line pattern: `U|| U|U ||| U|| U|| U|U | U` (20 syllables per line)

## 8. యతి (Yati) Matching

యతి is the caesura — a sound-match between the line's first akshar and an akshar at a specific position within the line.

### Yati Position Modes

| Mode | How Position is Determined | Used By |
|------|---------------------------|---------|
| **CharPosition** | Nth character/akshar in the line | వృత్తం meters (e.g., 10th syllable in ఉత్పలమాల) |
| **GPosition** | First akshar of the Nth గణం | జాతి meters (e.g., 4th గణం in కందం) |

### Two-Phase Matching Algorithm

The engine checks యతి in **two phases**: first a consonant/vowel match, then (for consonant matches) a finish-group confirmation. Both must pass.

#### Phase 1: Consonant or Vowel Match

Checked **in order** — first match wins:

| Step | Name | Condition | Match Logic |
|------|------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | **ౠత్వసౌమ్యవళి** | Both contain ృ | Always match |
| 2 | **ఋత్వసంబంధవళి** | One has ృ | Other must be in {ఇ,ఈ,ఋ,ౠ,ఎ,ఏ,ి,ీ,ృ,ౄ,ె,ే} |
| 3 | **ఋవళి** | One has ఋ or ృ | Other must start with ర and end with {ి,ీ,ె,ే} |
| 4 | **స్వరప్రధానవళి** | Extract vowels/finishes | Must be in same VowelAndFinishGroup |
| 5 | **ప్రత్యేక యతి** | One consonant is య or హ | Other must contain అ |
| 6 | **సమాన హల్లు** | Extract consonants | Exact same consonant found |
| 7 | **ConsoGroups** | Extract consonants | At least one consonant from each side in same group |
| 8 | **విశేషవళి** | One contains ఙ | Other must be ంక, ంఖ, ంగ, or ంఘ |
| 9 | **తద్భవవ్యాజ విశ్రమములు** | One has న or ణ | Other must contain ఙ |
| 10 | **బిందు యతులు** | One starts with ం | ం+consonant matched against nasal of that consonant's వర్గ |
| 11 | **Finish group confirmation** | Steps 6-10 matched | **Required second check** — see Phase 2 |
| 12 | **మువిభక్తియతి / ముకారయతి** | One is మ+{ు,ూ,ొ,ో} | Other must be {ప,ఫ,భ,బ,మ}+{ు,ూ,ొ,ో} |
| 13 | **అంత్యోష్మ సంధి కళులు** | One contains హ | Other has a specific aspirate conjunct |
| 14 | **SoundexSandhi** | Opt-in flag, **off by default** | Match FinishGroups only |

#### Phase 2: Finish Group Confirmation

After a consonant match (steps 6-10), the engine **also checks** vowel marking compatibility.

| Finish Group | Members | Compatible with no-finish? |
|-------------|---------|---------------------------|
| **అ-group** | ా, ై, ౌ, ం | Yes — no finish defaults to అ-group |
| **ఇ-group** | ి, ీ, ృ, ౄ, ె, ే | No |
| **ఉ-group** | ు, ూ, ొ, ో | No |

**Rules:**
- If neither side has a finish → match (both are అ-group by default)
- If one side has no finish, the other must be in the **అ-group**
- If both have finishes, they must be in the **same group**

### Consonant Groups (ConsoGroups)

| Group Name | Letters |
|-----------|---------|
| **క-వర్గ** | క, ఖ, గ, ఘ |
| **చ-వర్గ** | చ, ఛ, జ, ఝ |
| **ట-వర్గ** | ట, ఠ, డ, ఢ |
| **త-వర్గ** | త, థ, ద, ధ |
| **ప-వర్గ** | ప, ఫ, బ, భ |
| **ఋజు యతులు** | య, హ |
| **ఏకతరయతులు** | ర, ఱ |
| **అభేద విరతులు** | వ-బ, ల-ళ, ల-డ |
| **ఊష్మ విశ్రాంతులు** | శ, ష, స |
| **సరసయతి** | చ-ఛ-జ-ఝ-శ-ష-స |
| **సరసయతి** | న, ణ |
| **Special** | ల, ళ, డ, ర |

### Vowel/Finish Groups (VowelAndFinishGroups)

| Group | Vowels | Finishes |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **అ-group** | అ, ఆ, ఐ, ఔ | ా, ై, ౌ |
| **ఇ-group** | ఇ, ఈ, ఋ, ౠ, ఎ, ఏ | ి, ీ, ృ, ౄ, ె, ే |
| **ఉ-group** | ఉ, ఊ, ఒ, ఓ | ు, ూ, ొ, ో |

## 9. ప్రాస (Prasa) Matching

ప్రాస is the **2nd akshar** (syllable) of each line. All lines must share the same consonant at this position.

### Matching Algorithm

1. **Vowel bypass** — If either prasa akshar is a pure vowel, match succeeds
2. **Extract consonants** — Compare using PrasaGroups
3. **సంయుతాసంయుత ప్రాసము** — If one has 2 consonants and the 2nd is ల or ర, drop it and retry
4. **శాంతిప్రాసము** — First OR last consonant matches (if enabled)

### Prasa Consonant Groups

| Group Name | Letters |
|-----------|---------|
| ప్రాసవైరము | ఱ, ర |
| ప్రాది ప్రాసము | న, ణ |
| శ-ప్రాసము | స, ష, శ |
| అభేద ప్రాస | ల, ళ, డ, ర |
| అభేద ప్రాస | ప, వ |
| స్వవర్గజ ప్రాసము | క-గ, ఖ-ఘ, చ-జ, ట-డ, త-ద, ప-బ, etc. (15 pairs) |

## 10. Meter-Specific Constraints

| Constraint | Applies To | What It Means |
|------------|-----------|---------------|
| **OddNonJa** | కందం, other జాతి | Odd-positioned గణాలు must NOT be జ (`\|U\|`) |
| **Sixth rule** | కందం even lines | 3rd గణం in even lines must be జ or నల only |
| **GCount** | All meters | Each line must have exactly the specified number of గణాలు |
| **Weight** | జాతి meters | Each గణం must have specified matra count (typically 4) |
| **Character count** | వృత్తం meters | Each line must have exactly N syllables |

# Part II: Workflow

Step-by-step process for planning a Telugu శతకం.

---

## Phase 1: Brainstorm

**Goal:** Explore the theme, audience, and tone. Generate మకుటం candidates and select a chandam.

### Step 1: Theme Exploration

1. **What is the శతకం about?** — Identify the central theme (నీతి, భక్తి, వైరాగ్యం, శృంగారం, సామాజిక, ప్రకృతి, తత్త్వం, or a novel theme)
2. **Who is the audience?** — Scholars, general readers, children, devotees?
3. **What tone?** — Serious, playful, devotional, satirical, contemplative?
4. **What is the core message?** — One sentence that captures the entire శతకం's purpose

### Step 2: మకుటం Ideation

Generate 3-5 candidate మకుటం phrases. For each, consider:

| Criterion | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| **Identity** | Does it capture the essence of the theme? |
| **Sound** | Is it melodious and memorable? |
| **Length** | How much of the last line does it consume? (shorter = more freedom) |
| **Grammar** | Can it follow 108 different sentences naturally? |
| **Meter** | What guru/laghu pattern does it have? Which meters can accommodate it? |

**Tip:** Vocative forms (సంబోధన) like "ఓ దేవా!", "నరసింహా!", "సుమతీ!" are the most flexible — they work as standalone addresses after any statement.

### Step 3: Chandam Selection

1. Run `list_rules` to see available meters
2. For each candidate meter, run `get_rule_info` to understand the pattern
3. Check: can the మకుటం fit the last line's pattern?
4. Run `get_examples` to see how classical poets used the meter

Present 2-3 candidate chandams with:

| Factor | Evaluation |
|--------|------------|
| మకుటం fit | Does the మకుటం align with the last line pattern? |
| Expressive room | How many syllables/gaNAs are free per padyam? |
| Difficulty | Can 108 padyams be sustained in this meter? |
| Tradition | Is this meter commonly used for శతకం in this theme? |
| ప్రాస burden | How constrained is ప్రాస across 108 padyams? |

### Step 4: Count Decision

- Default: **108** (అష్టోత్తర శతం — most traditional)
- Discuss alternatives if the theme is narrower or broader
- Plan for 1-2 ఆవాహన (invocation) padyams at the start
- Plan for 1-2 ఫలశ్రుతి (benediction) padyams at the end

### Output
- Clear theme statement with audience and tone
- 3-5 మకుటం candidates with analysis
- 2-3 chandam candidates with pros/cons
- Recommended మకుటం + chandam combination
- Padyam count decision

## Phase 2: మకుటం & Chandam Lock

**Goal:** Finalize the మకుటం and chandam. Verify they work together. Document constraints.

### Step 1: Finalize మకుటం

Select one మకుటం from the candidates. Confirm:
- [ ] It captures the theme's essence
- [ ] It is melodious when repeated
- [ ] It works grammatically after diverse sentence endings
- [ ] Its guru/laghu pattern is verified

### Step 2: Verify Metrical Fit

1. Run `get_rule_info(rule_identifier="<chandam>", include_examples=true)`
2. Map the మకుటం's guru/laghu pattern onto the last line's gaNA pattern
3. Identify exactly which gaNAs the మకుటం occupies
4. Calculate the "free zone" — how many gaNAs/syllables are available before the మకుటం

```
Example verification:
Chandam: ఆటవెలది
Last line (line 4): odd-type line = 5 gaNAs of 4 mātrās each
మకుటం: "విశ్వదాభిరామ వినురవేమ"
Guru/laghu: U U | U U | U | | U U | U U U
Occupies: last ~3 gaNAs
Free zone: first ~2 gaNAs (8 mātrās = ~3-4 words)
```

### Step 3: Test with Sample Padyam

Compose one sample padyam with the మకుటం and validate:

```
try_match_chandam(
  poem_text="<sample padyam>",
  rule_identifier="<chandam>",
  match_yati=true,
  match_prasa=true
)
```

If it fails, diagnose and adjust the మకుటం or chandam choice.

### Step 4: Assess ప్రాస Flexibility

Since the మకుటం is fixed, check:
- What is the 2nd syllable of the last line's మకుటం portion?
- Does the మకుటం constrain ప్రాస for all 108 padyams?
- How many different ప్రాస consonants can work across the collection?

**Note:** In some meters (like ఆటవెలది), ప్రాస rules are relaxed. In others (like ఉత్పలమాల), every line must match. For 108 padyams, you need ample ప్రాస options.

### Step 5: Document the Constraint Sheet

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **ఛందస్సు** | Name, type, identifier |
| **మకుటం** | Full text |
| **మకుటం position** | Which gaNAs/syllables of last line |
| **Free zone** | Available gaNAs/syllables before మకుటం |
| **యతి position** | Where యతి falls in the last line (relative to మకుటం) |
| **ప్రాస constraints** | What consonants are viable across 108 padyams |
| **Meter-specific rules** | OddNonJa, Sixth, etc. if applicable |

### Output
- Confirmed మకుటం with guru/laghu analysis
- Verified metrical fit with sample padyam
- ప్రాస flexibility assessment
- Complete constraint sheet

## Phase 3: Sectional Architecture

**Goal:** Divide the padyams into logical sections with a coherent flow.

### Step 1: Define the Narrative Arc

Every great శతకం has an implicit structure, even if each padyam stands alone. Plan the overall arc:

```
Opening (ఆవాహన) → Foundation → Development → Depth → Climax → Resolution → Closing (ఫలశ్రుతి)
```

### Step 2: Design Sections

Divide the total count into **8-12 sections**. Each section is a thematic cluster.

**Template for each section:**

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Section name** | A descriptive Telugu name |
| **Verse range** | e.g., Padyams 5-16 |
| **Count** | Number of padyams in this section |
| **Sub-theme** | What aspect of the main theme does this section explore? |
| **Emotional tone** | What feeling should the reader experience? |
| **Key concepts** | 3-5 central ideas for this section |
| **Anchor padyam** | Which padyam(s) carry the most weight? |

### Step 3: Plan Transitions

Between sections, identify:
- **Bridge padyams** — Verses that transition from one sub-theme to the next
- **Contrast points** — Where the tone shifts deliberately
- **Callbacks** — Later sections that reference or deepen earlier themes

### Step 4: Balance Check

| Check | Question |
|-------|----------|
| **Coverage** | Does every important aspect of the theme get addressed? |
| **Balance** | Are sections roughly similar in length? (±2-3 padyams is fine) |
| **Progression** | Does the reader experience a journey from start to end? |
| **Variety** | Do sections use different approaches (narrative, philosophical, imagery-based, dialogic)? |
| **No repetition** | Are there duplicate concepts across sections? Merge or differentiate them |

### Example Section Map

```
శతకం: నీతి శతకం (108 padyams)

Section 1: ఆవాహన (1-3) — Invocation, blessing
Section 2: విద్య మహిమ (4-15) — Importance of education and learning
Section 3: సజ్జన లక్షణాలు (16-27) — Qualities of good people
Section 4: దుర్జన లక్షణాలు (28-39) — Traits of the wicked (contrast)
Section 5: ధన విషయం (40-51) — Wisdom about wealth and poverty
Section 6: రాజనీతి (52-63) — Governance and leadership
Section 7: కుటుంబ ధర్మం (64-75) — Family, relationships, duty
Section 8: కాల మహిమ (76-87) — Time, impermanence, fate
Section 9: ఆధ్యాత్మికత (88-99) — Spiritual wisdom, inner peace
Section 10: సమాపనం (100-105) — Summary, final wisdom
Section 11: ఫలశ్రుతి (106-108) — Benediction, fruit of reading
```

### Output
- Complete section map with all fields filled
- Transition plan between sections
- Balance verification notes

## Phase 4: Per-Padyam Planning

**Goal:** Plan the concept, idea, and key elements for each padyam. Use flexible detail — anchor padyams get full treatment, others get concise one-liners.

### Detail Levels

| Level | When to Use | What to Include |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Full** | Anchor padyams, opening, closing, transitions | Concept + key words/phrases + imagery/metaphor + connection to adjacent verses |

| **Minimal** | Supporting/filler padyams | One-line concept description |

### Full Detail Template (for anchor padyams)

```markdown
### Padyam #N — [Short Title]
- **Concept:** [Core idea]
- **Key image:** [One image or phrase]
```

### Minimal Detail Template

```markdown

```

### Planning Process

For each section:
1. List all padyam numbers in the section
2. Identify the anchor padyam(s) — plan these first at Full detail
3. Plan the opening and closing padyam of the section at Standard detail
4. Fill remaining padyams at Minimal or Standard detail
5. Review for logical flow within the section
6. Check: no concept repeats another padyam elsewhere in the శతకం

### Quality Checks Per Section

- [ ] Each padyam has a distinct concept (no duplicates)
- [ ] The anchor padyam is clearly the strongest idea
- [ ] Flow within section is logical (not random)
- [ ] Variety in approach (not all padyams use the same structure)
- [ ] Key theme vocabulary is distributed, not clustered

# Part III: Composition Bridge

When ready to compose individual padyams from this plan, follow these steps for each padyam:

1. **Pick a padyam** from the plan — start with anchor padyams
2. **Extract constraints** from the constraint sheet (chandam, ప్రాస, యతి, మకుటం position)
3. **Draft the padyam** — write the last line first (since మకుటం is fixed), then work backwards
4. **Validate** using `try_match_chandam`
5. **Iterate** until the padyam passes validation
6. **Record** the composed padyam alongside its plan entry

### Composition Tip: Write the Last Line First

Since the మకుటం is fixed, always start composing from the last line:
1. The మకుటం occupies the end of the last line — this is given
2. Fill the "free zone" before the మకుటం with content matching the padyam's concept
3. Ensure ప్రాస matches the other lines you'll write
4. Then draft lines 1-3 (or 1-N-1), working to satisfy ప్రాస, యతి, and meaning

This "last line first" approach prevents the most common శతకం pitfall: drafting beautiful opening lines only to find the మకుటం won't fit naturally at the end.
