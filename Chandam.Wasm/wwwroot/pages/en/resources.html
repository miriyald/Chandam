Resources

A logically organized collection for learning, researching, and practicing Telugu prosody (ఛందస్సు).

1) Core References & Trusted Overviews

  • ఛందస్సు — Telugu Wikipedia — Basic definitions, gana system, and classifications.
  • Sanskrit Encyclopaedia of Prosody — Broad reference for classical meters and terminology.
  • Sanskrit Documents (Telugu script) — Classical works, including chandas-related texts in Telugu script.

2) Telugu Literature Sources & Digital Libraries

  • Andhra Bharati — Telugu literature portal with reference material and dictionary support.
  • TeluguBhagavatam (Potana Bhagavatam) — Rich poetic corpus useful for meter study.
  • TeluguThesis — Digital access to Telugu and Sanskrit books.
  • Sundarayya Digital Telugu Books — Free archive of Telugu publications.

3) Books, PDFs, and Textual Study

  • Chhando Vyakaranamu (PDF / Archive) — Traditional text covering prosody, grammar, and alankara basics.
  • Chandoratnavali (Digital Book) — Explanations and examples across many Telugu meters.
  • Kovela Sampathkumaracharya — Chandahpadakosam (print reference work).
  • Masters' Telugu Grammar (widely used companion reference for prosody topics).

4) Video Lessons & YouTube Learning

  • Telugu Chandassu Classes (Playlist) — Step-by-step lessons from basics to intermediate level.
  • Chandassu Part-1 — Intro lesson for beginners.
  • Class 10 Chandassu Lesson — Guru/Laghu, gana patterns, yati, and prasa.
  • Telugu Online Teaching (Channel) — Multiple lessons on prosody and Telugu grammar.

5) Dictionaries & Term Lookup

  • Prosody meaning (English–Telugu) — quick terminology support.
  • Shabdkosh — Prosody — multilingual definitions and usage.
  • Andhra Bharati Dictionary — Telugu word meanings and related lexical help.

6) Communities, Social Discovery, and Collaboration

  • Chandam on GitHub — open-source code, discussions, and contributions.
  • Facebook search: “తెలుగు ఛందస్సు” — public groups, posts, and communities.
  • YouTube search: “తెలుగు ఛందస్సు” — latest classes, lectures, and discussions.

7) Useful Built-in Chandam Tools

  • Browse Rule Sets — Explore Telugu meter definitions and examples in the app.
  • Create Custom Rule — Build and test your own metrical patterns.
