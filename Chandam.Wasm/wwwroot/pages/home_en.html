Chandam Analysis

Analyze the meter of Telugu and Sanskrit poems with ease.

Legacy

This project started in 2007 as a simple idea. Over the years, it has evolved into a powerful analysis tool for Telugu and Sanskrit poetry. The project has helped in the digital preservation and analysis of poems.

New Objectives

Our new objectives are to improve the user experience using modern technology. We aim to increase accuracy, add more meter rules, and provide support for mobile devices. We also plan to collaborate with academics and researchers.

Features

  • 379+ Telugu meter rules
  • 1300+ Sanskrit meter rules
  • Gana division and Yati, Prasa analysis
  • Detailed error reporting
  • Editor for composing new poems
